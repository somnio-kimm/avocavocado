Goethe-Institut adult A1, A2 and B1 word lists are used only as factual headword/level references. Their publication copyright is not relicensed. No PDF pages or Goethe example sentences are included. Matching uses normalised explicit headwords and thematic groups, not a claim of exhaustive coverage of every productive word family. The earliest matching list determines A1–B1. Unmatched headwords use editorial frequency bands (B2: Zipf >= 4; C1: >= 3; C2: < 3).
